MEIDS-UAT-20261023 synthetic evidence note

Scenario: A release recommendation relies on an eight-month-old operational report and an unverified verbal statement. The change affects production access and cannot be reversed within the maintenance window.

Expected application: Pause the release. The accountable decision owner is the release lead. The evidence owner must obtain a current operational report and verify the verbal statement independently. The reviewer records the conflict and sets a review date. Work resumes only after the gap is closed or the decision owner explicitly accepts the residual risk.

Quality checks:
- Topic: evidence escalation for release decisions.
- Why: prevent decisions based on stale or incomplete evidence.
- How: identify owner, verify two independent signals, pause, assign evidence work, review.
- Who: release lead, evidence owner, independent reviewer.
- And then: either close the gap and resume or record explicit residual-risk acceptance.
